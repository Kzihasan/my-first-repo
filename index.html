Bangladesh

Bangladesh is a densely populated South Asian country located in the fertile delta of the Padma and Jamuna rivers, bordered by India and Myanmar. Officially the People's Republic of Bangladesh, it is the eighth-most populous country globally, with a population of over 170 million people and a rich cultural heritage.

New zealand

New Zealand is an island country in the southwestern Pacific Ocean, known for its diverse landscapes, from active volcanoes and glaciers to beaches and fjords. It consists of two main islands, North Island and South Island, with Wellington as the capital and Auckland as the largest city.
